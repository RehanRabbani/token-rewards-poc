# Token rewards in your app: a pilot in 6 weeks

**In one line:** in 6 weeks, a first group of your users earns reward tokens on every purchase and spends them as a discount on the next one, inside your app, with nothing new to learn.

## What your users get

- **They buy, they earn.** Every confirmed purchase gives tokens back (we suggest 2 percent of the price).
- **They spend tokens as a discount** at checkout, on their next order.
- **They see a balance, nothing else.** No wallet to set up, no fees, no crypto words. Card payments stay exactly as they are today.

## What you get

- **A reason for customers to come back.** They hold a balance that they can only spend with you.
- **A real token on a public blockchain.** Every token given and spent is on a record you can check yourselves, and it is the base for bigger steps later.
- **A safe start.** 5 to 10 percent of your users first, a monthly budget that you set, and one switch that pauses everything.
- **Numbers to decide with.** After 2 to 4 weeks you see how many users earned and redeemed, and what it cost. Then we decide together to open it to everyone.

## The 6 weeks

| Weeks | What happens | What you decide |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | We agree the reward rules and build the core. Your lawyers review the token | The scope, and your lawyers' yes to the token |
| 3–4 | Your app shows the balance and the discount. We test everything that can go wrong | That it works in your test app |
| 5–6 | You test it, the app stores approve it, your support team is trained | **Go live with the pilot** |

**The date holds.** If your lawyers need more time, the same pilot launches on the same date with points, and we switch the token on when they approve.

The hardest part is already built: keeping your app's balances and the blockchain in agreement when a payment arrives twice, a checkout is repeated, or an order is refunded. We can show it to you working.

## Not in the first 6 weeks

Sending tokens to other users, selling or cashing them out, and paying with crypto. These fall under different laws in most countries, so they are the second phase. We plan it with you once the pilot is live.

## What we need from you

1. One person who decides on scope, and your lawyers from week 1.
2. Payment and refund notifications from your payment provider, by week 2.
3. One mobile and one backend engineer, part-time, from week 3.
4. Your reward rules: the rate, the monthly budget, and when tokens expire (we suggest 12 months).

## This proposal assumes

"Pay with a token" means spending earned tokens as a discount. Tokens are earned, not bought. "Launch" means a pilot first. You fund the rewards. **If any of these is wrong, tell us on the first call,** because it changes the plan.
