# Token rewards in your app: a pilot in 6 weeks

**In one line:** in 6 weeks, a first group of your users earns reward tokens on every purchase and spends them as a discount on the next one, inside your app, with nothing new to learn.

## What your users get

- **They buy, they earn.** Every confirmed purchase gives tokens back (we suggest 2 percent).
- **They spend tokens as a discount** on their next order.
- **They see a balance, nothing else.** No wallet, no fees, no crypto words. Card payments stay as they are today.

## What you get

- **A reason to come back.** Customers hold a balance they can only spend with you.
- **A token you can check.** Every token given and spent is on a public blockchain record you can verify yourselves.
- **A safe start.** 5 to 10 percent of users first, a monthly budget you set, and one switch that pauses everything.
- **Numbers to decide with.** After 2 to 4 weeks you see who earned, who redeemed, and what it cost. Then we decide together to open it to everyone.

## How it works

Your app → our rewards service → a token on a public blockchain

1. Your payment system tells our service about each purchase and refund. Our service keeps the ledger (who earned and spent what) and answers your app at once.
2. A background worker copies each change to the blockchain. If the blockchain is slow or down, your app keeps working and the copy catches up.
3. A daily check compares the ledger with the blockchain and reports any difference.
4. Users see a balance only. Wallets and keys stay with us. If you want a say, you can hold the admin key: it lets you replace our key but cannot create tokens. It controls the token, not our ledger.

The hardest part is already built: keeping your balances and the blockchain in agreement when a payment repeats or an order is refunded. We can show it working.

## The 6 weeks

| Weeks | What happens | Milestone: what you decide |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | We agree the reward rules and build the core. Your lawyers review the token | The scope, and your lawyers' yes to the token |
| 3–4 | Your app shows the balance and the discount. We test everything that can go wrong | That it works in your test app |
| 5–6 | You test it, the app stores approve it, your support team is trained | **Go live with the pilot** |

**The date holds.** If your lawyers need more time, the same pilot launches on the same date with points, and we switch the token on when they approve.

## Not in the first 6 weeks

Sending tokens between users, selling or cashing them out, and paying with crypto. Different laws apply in most countries, so these are phase 2, planned with you once the pilot is live.

## What we need from you to build it

1. One person who decides on scope, and your lawyers from week 1.
2. Payment and refund notifications from your payment provider, by week 2. Each one needs a unique ID and a stable user ID, so that a repeated message never rewards twice.
3. A test version of your app, and one mobile and one backend engineer, part-time, from week 3. We give them a ready-made kit and a fake copy of our service so they can start early.
4. Your reward rules: the rate, the monthly budget, and when tokens expire (we suggest 12 months).

## This proposal assumes

"Pay with a token" means spending earned tokens as a discount. Tokens are earned, not bought. "Launch" means a pilot first. You fund the rewards. **If any of these is wrong, tell us on the first call,** because it changes the plan.
