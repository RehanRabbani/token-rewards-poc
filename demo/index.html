Rewards demo: the ledger and the blockchain, side by side

Left: what the partner's app does. Right: what our ledger says and what the blockchain says. The proof is that the two never disagree without us knowing, even when things go wrong.

1. The partner's app

The payment provider tells us the payment is confirmed. We record the reward in the ledger and answer at once. The blockchain follows.

1 point = 1 cent of discount. To refund an order, use the button next to it in the ledger table below.

2. Things that go wrong

The payment message arrives twice. Payment providers retry. The customer must be rewarded once.

Five checkouts at the same moment. Each one tries to spend the whole balance. Only one may succeed.

The blockchain is slow or down. The app keeps working from the ledger. The chain catches up later.

Points appear on the chain that the ledger never recorded. A bug or a stolen key. The check must find it. It reports and does not repair, so this customer stays red.
